# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages, product showcase, updates, Pro offer, creator page, and public game-profile pages.
- Search, social-preview, and AI-crawler visibility from source-visible HTML.
- Source-detectable WCAG 2.2 Level A/AA issues on public pages.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated optimizer, account, payment-return, and admin routes.
- Desktop/Tauri-only application screens.

## Target audience
- Windows 10/11 gamers seeking hardware-aware PC and game optimization, especially for FiveM, Fortnite, Call of Duty, Rust, and Roblox.

## Primary keywords
- Windows gaming PC optimizer
- FPS optimization and game-specific Windows optimization
- FiveM, Fortnite, Call of Duty, Rust, and Roblox optimization
- Opti Gods and leaq

## Crawler assumptions
- Public marketing content should be available in initial HTML for search, social-preview, and AI crawlers that do not execute JavaScript.
- Public routes intentionally promoted in the sitemap or game-profile hub should be indexable unless explicitly excluded.
